Turning software into evidence, and evidence into better software.

ARIES Lab conducts rigorous, applied research in Software Engineering, producing evidence that advances both scientific knowledge and software practice, from testing and refactoring to AI-assisted development and developer tools.

Research lines

Advancing Software Engineering
through research.

Our research is organized around five complementary areas, connecting rigorous academic investigation with challenges faced in software development and practice.

Software Testing

Investigates methods, techniques, and tools for improving the effectiveness, maintainability, and quality of software testing.

Sustainable Software Engineering

Explores how software can be designed, developed, and evolved with sustainability in mind, considering technical, environmental, and long-term concerns.

Empirical Software Engineering

Applies quantitative and qualitative methods to build evidence about software development, developers, processes, and technologies.

AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE)

Explores the use of artificial intelligence to understand, support, and improve software engineering activities, from development and maintenance to testing and quality assurance.

Software Evolution

Investigates how software systems change over time, with a focus on understanding, maintaining, and improving evolving codebases.
